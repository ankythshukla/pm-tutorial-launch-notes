# PRD: Quick Reorder

## Problem
40% of our orders are repeat orders, but users take 6 taps to reorder the same meal.

## Goal
Let users reorder their last meal in 2 taps from the home screen.

## Success metric
Repeat-order conversion goes up from 18% to 22% in 30 days.

## Guardrail metric
Average order value (AOV) should not drop by more than 3%. If it does, we pause the rollout.

## Out of scope
Scheduled orders, group orders, reordering from a closed restaurant.
